## About this site

**Linkawho?** is inspired by *Jerry's Guide to the World Wide Web*, the hand-curated directory that later became Yahoo!. Like the original guide, **Linkawho?** aims to help people discover interesting corners of Internet compatible with legacy PCs through human curation rather than algorithms.
